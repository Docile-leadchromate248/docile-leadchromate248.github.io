What is shotmark?

Shotmark helps you capture your screen and add marks to images. You can draw shapes, highlight text, and hide sensitive details using the mosaic tool. The application works locally on your computer. It does not send your images to any servers, which keeps your data private.

🚀 How to get started

Follow these steps to install the software on your Windows computer.

  1. Visit the official releases page.
  2. Find the file ending in .exe for Windows.
  3. Click the file name to start your download.
  4. Open the file once the download finishes.
  5. Follow the instructions on your screen to complete the setup process.

✨ Main features

  • Fast Captures: Take a shot of your screen instantly.
  • Drawing Tools: Add arrows, lines, and shapes to your images.
  • Mosaic Tool: Blur out private information like names or addresses.
  • Privacy First: Shotmark never uploads your files. Everything stays on your local drive.

🛠 System Requirements

Shotmark works on most modern Windows computers. Ensure you have at least 200MB of free disk space and a working mouse or trackpad. The software requires a standard Windows 10 or Windows 11 installation to run without issues.

❓ Common Questions

Where do my images go? Your images stay in the folder you specify on your computer hard drive.

Does this connect to the internet? No. The application performs all actions offline for your security.

Can I change the colors of my drawings? Yes. Select the shape tool and use the settings panel to pick a color.
